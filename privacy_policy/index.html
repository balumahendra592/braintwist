Privacy Policy

Brain Twist — Tricky Puzzle Game

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Brain Twist ("we", "our", or "us") is developed by balaregeti. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use the Brain Twist mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information directly. All game progress (completed levels, coins, stars, settings) is stored locally on your device only using Android's SharedPreferences and never transmitted to our servers.


The following information may be collected automatically by our third-party partners:


Google AdMob

Brain Twist displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage data and interaction with ads
  • Device model, OS version, and language

This data is used to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Google Analytics for Firebase (Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to understand how players use the App. This may collect:

  • App open and session data
  • Screen views and user engagement events
  • Crash and performance data
  • Device type and OS version

Data is aggregated and anonymised. No individual user is identified. See Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

2. How We Use Information

  • To display advertisements and generate revenue that keeps the App free
  • To analyse gameplay patterns and improve the App
  • To diagnose crashes and fix technical issues

We do not sell, trade, or share any personal information with third parties beyond the services described above.

3. Advertising & Ad Choices

Brain Twist shows three types of Google AdMob ads:

  • Banner ads — displayed at the bottom of game screens
  • Interstitial ads — shown between levels (every 3 levels)
  • Rewarded video ads — optional; watch to earn hint coins

You can opt out of personalised ads by resetting your Android Advertising ID or enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" in your device settings under Google → Ads.

4. Data Storage & Retention

All game data (progress, coins, settings) is stored locally on your device only using Android SharedPreferences. This data is never transmitted to external servers. You can delete all local data at any time by uninstalling the App or using the "Reset Progress" option in the Settings screen.

Data collected by Google AdMob and Analytics is retained and processed by Google in accordance with their respective data retention policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Brain Twist is rated Everyone (3+) and is suitable for all ages. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. AdMob is configured to request non-personalised ads where applicable under COPPA and similar regulations. If you believe a child has inadvertently provided personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will take steps to remove it.

6. Third-Party Services

This App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Analytics for Firebase

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content or practices of these services.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of personalised advertising

Since we do not directly collect or store personal data, most requests should be directed to Google. For App-specific data, uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
